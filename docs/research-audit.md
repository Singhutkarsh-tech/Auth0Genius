## Quality guardrails

- Claim references must point to saved sources. “Verified” claims require an official source; third-party pages are labeled.
- Durations are engineering estimates with assumptions, not provider promises. External waiting time is unknown unless established.
- Live source IDs and links are supplied by the server, never accepted as invented LLM citations.
- Structural validation cannot prove a source semantically supports every sentence. The UI discloses this limit, and human review remains important.
- Search and fetched pages are treated as untrusted data. No scripts or document instructions are executed.

Live-run and automated validation results are recorded separately in [validation.md](validation.md).

## P1 remediation evidence policy

The initial source-access timestamps above are historical and were not rewritten. Remediation re-opened the official Composio managed/custom and authentication pages and the Gmail, Slack and GitHub policy references. Seed claims no longer become verified merely because they contain citation IDs. The top-level OAuth, managed and conditional approval classifications have explicit reviewed claim bindings; general setup suggestions remain labeled unverified unless individually reviewed. GitHub’s organization-approval condition is separated from any unsupported universal review exemption.

Live captures store actual fetched text and content hashes, with original and final URLs. Search snippets are used only for discovery. Topic tags and authority are relevance gates, not entailment checks. Model output cannot assign its own semantic review: live proposals remain unresolved, and positive summary classifications are downgraded to unknown before publication. This intentionally trades automatic certainty for a partial, inspectable research result. No second-model verdict is treated as proof.

Known cross-domain provider documentation mappings are reviewed code data, not arbitrary domains returned by the model. Missing mappings and source meaning still require review. See the P1 remediation section in [validation.md](validation.md) for the actual live-run outcome, including failed attempts.
